Acme Corp Engineering Onboarding

Day one. Every new joiner gets a laptop configured before they arrive, a buddy from their own
team, and a 30-day checklist owned by their manager. The buddy is not the manager and is not
expected to be technical support; the point is somebody to ask the questions nobody writes down.

Accounts. Access is granted by team membership rather than individually, so joining a team grants
what that team has and leaving revokes it. Requests for individual exceptions go through the
engineering manager and are reviewed quarterly.

First contribution. The expectation is a merged change in the first week. It is deliberately not
expected to be significant — the goal is to prove the path from a local checkout to production
works and that somebody will review your code, not to deliver anything.

Training budget. Every engineer has an annual budget of 60,000 INR for courses, books and
conferences, and two working days a month for learning. Unused budget does not carry over.

Probation. Six months, with a written review at three. Nothing in the review should ever be a
surprise; if it is, the manager has already failed.
