# Acme Corp People Policies

## Leave

Annual leave is 24 working days. Leave of three days or fewer needs two working days of notice.
Leave longer than three days needs two weeks of notice. Notice is waived for sick leave and
bereavement leave.

Sick leave is 12 days a year and does not carry over. A medical certificate is required from
the third consecutive day.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers get 26 weeks at full pay. Secondary caregivers get 8 weeks at full pay,
which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year.

## Working hours

Core hours are 11:00 to 17:00 IST. Outside core hours the schedule is yours. Fully remote
employees are expected on site for two weeks a year, funded by the company.

## Expenses

Expenses under 5,000 INR need no pre-approval and are reimbursed within one pay cycle.
Anything above needs manager approval in advance. Client entertainment is capped at
3,000 INR per head.
