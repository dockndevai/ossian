Acme Corp Engineering Handbook

Deployment windows. Production deploys are allowed Monday through Thursday between 10:00 and
16:00 IST. Friday deploys are forbidden except for Sev-1 incident fixes, which need sign-off
from the on-call lead. Deploys during a code freeze need VP Engineering approval.

On-call rotation. Each rotation lasts seven days and starts on Wednesday at 11:00 IST. The
secondary on-call is the person who held primary the previous week. Handover notes are
mandatory and go in the rotation channel before 11:00.

Incident severity. Sev-1 means customer-facing outage or data loss. Sev-2 means degraded
service with a workaround. Sev-3 is everything else. Sev-1 and Sev-2 require a written
postmortem within five working days.

Code review. Every change needs one approval, and two for anything touching authentication,
billing, or the migration directory. Approvals expire if the branch is force-pushed.

Laptop refresh. Engineering laptops are refreshed every three years. A replacement before that
needs a written justification to the engineering manager.
