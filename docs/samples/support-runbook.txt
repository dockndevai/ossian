Acme Corp Customer Support Runbook

Refunds. Enterprise customers may request a refund within 30 days of the invoice date.
Self-serve customers have a 14 day window. Refunds after the window need finance approval and
are issued as account credit, not to the original payment method.

Escalation. First response is due within 4 business hours for enterprise and 1 business day
for self-serve. If a ticket is untouched for 2 business days it escalates to the support lead
automatically.

Data export requests. A customer may request a full export of their data at any time. Export
is delivered within 7 days as newline-delimited JSON over a signed URL that expires in 72 hours.

Account deletion. Deletion requests are honoured within 30 days. Backups are purged on the
normal 90 day rotation, which is disclosed in the privacy policy.
